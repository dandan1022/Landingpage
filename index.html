How Palengke Express works

From market stall to doorstep.

A simple order flow keeps the customer, seller and driver synchronized.

1Customer selects a seller and adds fresh goods to the basket.
2Customer places an order using an exact map pin and delivery address.
3Seller accepts, prepares the order and books a driver.
4Driver accepts the delivery and updates progress until arrival.
5Customer confirms the order was actually delivered.
